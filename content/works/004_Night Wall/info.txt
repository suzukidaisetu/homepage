# 暗さで描く場所
夜の公園に、一枚の長い壁を置く習作。

照明を最小限にし、壁に当たるわずかな光と木々の影だけで場所の輪郭をつくる。昼には見えない、暗さによって立ち上がる空間を探った。

--- en
# A Place Drawn in Darkness
A study that places one long wall in a park at night.

Lighting is kept to a minimum, so the place takes shape only from the faint light on the wall and the shadows of the trees. It looks for a space that appears through darkness and cannot be seen by day.
