# 点だけで描く
点描だけで描いた列柱廊のドローイング。

線を使わず点の密度だけで光と影を表すと、建物は輪郭を失い、空気の濃淡として現れてくる。描き方そのものを設計の手段として試した一枚。

--- en
# Drawn Only in Dots
A drawing of a colonnade made only with stippling.

Without lines, light and shadow come from the density of dots alone. The building loses its outline and appears as shades of air. The drawing tests a way of drawing as a way of designing.
