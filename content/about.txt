--- en
role: Architecture student / Takei Lab, KIT
I study architecture and keep this table as a record of what I make and what I look at. Drawings, renders, models and references sit side by side, so the links between them stay visible.

I am interested in how a city works as a coordinate system rather than a fixed geometry, and in the phenomena a place collects, such as light, fog and sound, more than in its objects. In design work I look for the work that already exists inside a building and try to make it visible.

Study: Takei Lab, KIT
Tools: Rhinoceros, ArchiCAD, Twinmotion
Interests: Cities and grids, Kyoto, phenomena, the work behind buildings
